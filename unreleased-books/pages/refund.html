Refund policy.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Pre-launch notice Checkout is not live yet. No payment can be taken through this site. This policy will apply once orders open.

Our 7-day guarantee

If a book is not what you expected, email us within 7 days of purchase and we will refund the amount you paid for that book. You do not need to give a reason, but telling us what went wrong helps us improve.

How to request a refund

Send an email to hello@unreleasedbooks.com with:

  • the email address used at checkout;
  • your order number, if you have it;
  • the title you want refunded.

We may ask for the order confirmation so we can locate the purchase. Please do not send full card details by email.

When refunds are issued

Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method. Processing usually takes 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or payment provider. We will confirm by email when the refund has been submitted.

Bundles and multiple books

If you bought a bundle, you can request a refund for the whole bundle within the guarantee period. Partial refunds for individual titles in a bundle are at our discretion.

Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under applicable consumer law. Where the law gives you a stronger right to cancel or obtain a remedy, that right applies.

Chargebacks

If something has gone wrong, please contact us before opening a chargeback. We can usually resolve the issue faster directly.

Questions about a refund?

Email hello@unreleasedbooks.com. We reply within 1–2 business days.
