Refunds

Last updated November 2026

Seven days

If the book is not what you expected, email within seven days of purchase and the payment will be refunded in full. You do not need to explain why.

How to ask

Write to hello@thegildedcode.com from the address you used at checkout, with the order reference if you have it. Refunds are returned to the original payment method.

How long it takes

Refunds are approved within two business days. Your bank or card provider usually takes another five to ten business days to show the money back.
