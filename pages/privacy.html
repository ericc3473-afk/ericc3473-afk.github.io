Privacy

Last updated November 2026

What this site collects

This is a static site. It does not set advertising cookies, run third-party analytics, or build a profile of you as you read.

Payments

When checkout is connected, payment is handled by a third-party provider such as Stripe, Lemon Squeezy or Gumroad. Your card details go to that provider directly and are never stored on this site. The provider’s own privacy policy applies to that transaction.

Email

If you email hello@thegildedcode.com, your address and message are used only to answer you and to deliver what you asked for. They are not sold or added to a marketing list without your agreement.

Hosting

The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may record standard server logs, such as IP address and requested page, as part of operating the service.

Your rights

You can ask what personal information is held about you, ask for a copy, or ask for it to be deleted. Write to the address above and it will be handled.

Contact

Questions about this page: hello@thegildedcode.com.
