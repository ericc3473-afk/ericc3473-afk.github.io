Legal Privacy

Privacy policy.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Pre-launch notice Checkout is not live yet. No payment can be taken through this site. This policy describes how we will handle information when orders open.

Who we are

Unreleased Books is an independent digital publisher. For privacy questions, contact hello@unreleasedbooks.com.

Information we collect

We aim to collect only what we need to sell and deliver a digital book:

  • Order details: your name, email address, billing details and the titles you purchased.
  • Payment information: handled by our payment provider. We do not store full card numbers on this site.
  • Support messages: the content of emails you send us and our replies.
  • Technical data: basic server logs such as IP address, browser type and pages requested, used for security and reliability.
  • Local cart data: your cart is stored in your browser using localStorage so it survives a page refresh.

How we use information

  • to process orders, deliver download links and provide support;
  • to prevent fraud, abuse and unauthorised access;
  • to meet accounting, tax and legal obligations;
  • to improve the site and understand which pages are useful.

Cookies and local storage

This site uses localStorage to remember your cart. It is not used for advertising. If we add analytics or marketing cookies later, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the store, such as payment processing, email delivery and hosting providers. They may process information on our behalf and are expected to protect it. We do not sell personal information.

Retention

We keep order and tax records for as long as required by law. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve the issue and maintain a record of the conversation. Cart data stays in your browser until you clear it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. You may also have the right to data portability and to complain to a data protection authority. To make a request, email us.

International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than yours. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for those transfers.

Children

The store is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes

We may update this policy as the store develops. The date at the top shows when it was last revised. Material changes will be announced on this page.

Privacy questions

Email hello@unreleasedbooks.com. We reply within 1–2 business days.
