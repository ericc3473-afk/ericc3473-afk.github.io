THE GILDED CODE - preview copy
==============================

Thanks for taking a look. This folder contains the complete website for
The Gilded Code: Abundance by Design by Kael Varnow.

OPEN IT ON WINDOWS
------------------
1. Double-click START-HERE-WINDOWS.bat
2. Your browser opens at http://localhost:8000
3. Keep the black window open while you browse. Close it when you are done.

OPEN IT ON MAC OR LINUX
-----------------------
1. Open Terminal in this folder.
2. Run: sh START-HERE-MAC-LINUX.sh
3. Your browser opens automatically.

If you double-click index.html instead, the site still works, but the
custom fonts may fall back to system fonts. The launcher gives you the
real thing, with the correct fonts and icons.

ABOUT THE CHECKOUT
------------------
The storefront is finished, but payments are not connected in this copy.
The buy button shows "Checkout is not connected yet" on purpose.
No payment can be taken and nothing is charged.

WHAT IS INSIDE

                       what's inside, and the buy section)
- pages/refund.html, privacy.html, terms.html
- assets/cover.jpg ... the cover artwork
